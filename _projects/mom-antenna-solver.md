For our Quantitative Engineering Analysis 2 final, Charlie Sands and I implemented a method of moments solver, designed and simulated an antenna in it, compared the results to MATLAB's built-in Antenna Toolbox, and built a physical model of the antenna for validation. We also created a short activity to teach other students how to design patch antennas.

**Collaborator:** *Charlie Sands*
