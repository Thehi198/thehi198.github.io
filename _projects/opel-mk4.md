## Magnetic circuit

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/mk4/field-validation-rig.jpg" alt="MK4 thruster body with a yellow validation channel installed" caption="MK4 with a validation channel rig. We validated the magnetic field against our COMSOL simulations using an in-house magnetic field mapper to confirm shielding." %}

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/mk4/electromagnet.jpg" alt="Gloved hands holding a small copper-wound electromagnet rod" caption="Our first in-house electromagnet. MK4 has four positive magnets along the outside and a single negative magnet in the center. Each rod is enamel-insulated copper wire with consistent turns per layer, wound on our in-house EM rod turner. The inner rod is wound in the opposite direction to reverse its polarity." %}

## Electrical isolation

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/mk4/isolation-flaw.jpg" alt="Thruster face glowing at the magnetic screen" caption="A design flaw left the anode improperly isolated, coupling plasma to the magnetic screen instead of confining it to the anode." %}

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/mk4/coating-jig.jpg" alt="Diffuser seated in a custom coating jig" caption="To fix the isolation, we coated the diffuser with boron nitride. The spray pools and chips easily, so I designed a jig that holds the diffuser and keeps the plenum and sides uncoated." %}

{% include figure.html src="/assets/images/mk4/coated-diffuser.jpg" alt="White boron-nitride-coated annular diffuser" caption="The diffuser after coating and post-processing. It keeps anode potential from reaching the rest of the system." %}
