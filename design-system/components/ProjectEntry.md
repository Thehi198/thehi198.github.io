Provide `title`, `href`, `index` (rendered two-digit), `year`, `role`, `org`, a one or two sentence `abstract` written as results, and up to four `tags`. Stack entries directly; each draws its own top hairline. Order by significance, not date. Do not add thumbnails inside the entry; put images on the project page as Figures.
